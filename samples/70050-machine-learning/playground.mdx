# Least squares, by hand

This page is **MDX**: Markdown with components placed anywhere in the text. It retells steps 1, 5 and 6 of

## The criterion

For a line $\hat y_i=w_0+w_1x_i$, the residual of point $i$ is $\epsilon_i=y_i-\hat y_i$, and the loss adds up
their squares:

$$
L(\mathbf w)=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\epsilon_i^2=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\big(y_i-w_0-w_1x_i\big)^2
$$

## Two equations, one matrix

Setting $\partial L/\partial w_0$ and $\partial L/\partial w_1$ to zero gives two linear equations in $w_0,w_1$.
Stacked, they are one matrix equation. The rows below are the same `Row` the structured topics use:

<Row

## The shapes

| Quantity | Shape | Why |
|---|---|---|
| $X$ | $N\times2$ | a column of 1s and a column of inputs |
| $X^\top X$ | $2\times2$ | $(2\times N)(N\times2)$ |
| $X^\top\mathbf y$ | $2\times1$ | $(2\times N)(N\times1)$ |
| $\hat{\mathbf w}$ | $2\times1$ | one weight per column |
